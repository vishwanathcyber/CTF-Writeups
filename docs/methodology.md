# CTF Methodology

## Overview

This document describes the systematic methodology used while solving Capture The Flag (CTF) challenges.

## General Process

1. Understand the challenge objective.
2. Perform reconnaissance and information gathering.
3. Identify potential vulnerabilities.
4. Develop an exploitation strategy.
5. Validate the solution.
6. Document the findings.
7. Reflect on lessons learned.

## Principles

- Think before executing tools.
- Understand why an exploit works.
- Prefer reproducible methods.
- Document every important observation.
